Mutable & variant analysis pipelines

A public database of 79,000+ curated de novo variants, plus pipelines for early-onset disease analysis

Mutable is a public, gene-centric database of 79,000+ curated de novo variants from published studies of neurodevelopmental disorders and congenital anomalies. I maintain and extend it. My work includes gene-based querying, variant-level annotation, and 3D visualization of spatial clustering among missense variants.

I also optimize variant analysis pipelines for database curation and clinical diagnostics:

  • Support for multiple input formats (FASTQ, BAM/CRAM, VCF)
  • Improved variant calling and quality control
  • Functional annotation with gene- and variant-level constraint scores to help identify rare disease variants in early-onset cases

Tools: Nextflow, SAMtools, BCFtools, FreeBayes, DeepVariant, Docker, AWS
