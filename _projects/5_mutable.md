- Maintained and extended [Mutable.wiki](https://mutable.wiki), a public, gene-centric database featuring 79,000+ curated _de novo_ variants from published studies of neurodevelopmental disorders and congenital anomalies; implemented gene-based querying, variant-level annotation, and 3D visualization of spatial clustering among missense variants.
- Optimized variant analysis pipelines for both database curation and clinical diagnostics, supporting multiple input formats (FASTQ, BAM/CRAM, VCF). Enhancements include improved variant calling, quality control, and functional annotation using gene- and variant-level constraint scores to support identification of rare disease variants in early-onset cases.
