Addressing the limited interpretability of noncoding variants by estimating their fitness impact from the human allele frequency spectrum, using RNA-binding protein (RBP) binding and cross-species conservation as priors; developing deep learning models that learn RBP binding across proteins to predict variant effects on post-transcriptional regulation. _(Ongoing.)_

- Training sequence models of RBP binding on ENCODE eCLIP data (168 RBPs) and benchmarking variant effect scores against human allele frequency and existing predictors (e.g., GPN-Star, AlphaGenome).
- Applying the model to _de novo_ variants in UTRs in autism and congenital heart disease cohorts to test for case enrichment and to relate predicted site disruption to selection coefficients outside conserved regions.
